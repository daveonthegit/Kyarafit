# Kyarafit Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 10, 2026

Kyarafit is a consumer cosplay wardrobe, build-tracking, and convention-planning app. This policy describes how Kyarafit handles your account information, cosplay-planning content, uploaded images, and local device cache data.

## Data Kyarafit collects

- Account identifiers used for sign-in, such as email address and profile name
- User-created content, including closet items, cosplay builds, convention plans, packing lists, and notes
- Images you choose to upload for items, builds, profile pictures, or progress tracking
- Device-local offline data and signed-in cache data needed to support offline or low-friction product use
- Limited subscription and billing metadata if paid plans are enabled in the future

## Data Kyarafit does not collect by default

- Precise location data
- Contacts or address book data
- Health, fitness, or medical data
- Advertising identifiers for targeted ads
- Cross-app or cross-site tracking data for third-party advertising
- Photos or files from your device unless you explicitly choose them for upload

Convention `location` fields in Kyarafit are user-entered text. The app does not collect device GPS location.

## How Kyarafit uses data

Kyarafit uses your data to:

- Create and secure your account
- Sync your cosplay-planning content across devices when you are signed in
- Store and display the images you upload
- Deliver verification emails and password-reset emails
- Operate product features such as builds, planning, and packing lists
- Support optional subscription management if paid plans are launched

## Local and cloud storage

On mobile devices, Kyarafit can store local offline data on the device. When you sign in, your Kyarafit profile, synced content, and uploaded images are stored with Kyarafit's backend infrastructure providers.

On web, limited browser-local auth/session state may be stored to support sign-in persistence.

## Service providers

Kyarafit currently relies on infrastructure and service providers for core operations. Depending on the feature you use, these may include:

- Convex for application backend, database, and file storage
- Better Auth for authentication flows
- Google or GitHub for optional social sign-in
- Resend for transactional emails such as verification and password reset
- Stripe for subscription or billing metadata if paid plans are launched
- Hosting providers used to serve the web app and supporting services

## Data sales and advertising

Kyarafit does not sell personal data. Kyarafit does not use third-party ad-tech tracking or cross-app tracking for targeted advertising.

## Permissions

Kyarafit requests photo-library access only when you choose to add or update an image. Kyarafit does not request contacts, microphone, Bluetooth, motion, or background location permissions for core app use.

## Account deletion

You can request deletion of your account from the app's account settings. Deleting your account permanently removes your cloud-synced Kyarafit profile data, cosplay-planning content, and uploaded images associated with that account, subject to any billing records that must be retained for legal or operational reasons.

Local-only data created while using the app without an account is separate from cloud-synced account data.
