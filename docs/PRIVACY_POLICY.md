# Kyarafit Privacy Policy

**Effective date: April 22, 2026**

The user-facing version of this policy is published in the web app at `/privacy` (see `web/src/app/privacy/page.tsx`).

Kyarafit is a cosplay wardrobe, build-tracking, and convention-planning app. This document summarizes the same commitments as the in-app policy.

## Data Kyarafit collects

- Account identifiers for sign-in (email, name, username, session data; social sign-in via Google or Apple when you choose it)
- User-created content: builds, wardrobe, convention plans, packing lists, notes
- Images you upload
- Device-local cache and offline data as needed for the product
- Limited billing metadata if paid plans are enabled

## Data we do not collect by default

- Precise GPS; contacts; health data; ad IDs for targeted ads; files you do not select for upload. Convention locations are user-entered text.

## How we use data

To run the Service, secure accounts, sync content, store uploads, send transactional email, and support optional billing.

## Service providers

May include Convex, Better Auth, Google or Apple (social sign-in), Resend, Stripe (if billing), and hosting providers. See the full policy for details.

## No sale of personal data; no ad tracking

Kyarafit does not sell personal information and does not use third-party ad-tech for cross-app targeted advertising.

## Account deletion

You can delete your account from in-app settings. Deletion removes cloud-synced profile and content subject to legal or billing record retention.
